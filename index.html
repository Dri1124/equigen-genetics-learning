YOUR GENETIC PLAN
46 chromosomes • 23 pairs
🏢
One chromosome in each pair comes from each biological parent. Together, the 23 pairs form your unique combination of genetic information.
🔀
Not simply a 50/50 copy of traits.
You inherit roughly half of your nuclear DNA from each biological parent, but before chromosomes are passed on, recombination can exchange DNA between paired chromosomes. This reshuffling helps make each person's genetic combination unique.

Think of each biological parent contributing one version of the plans for each chromosome pair. Your cells bring those inherited sets together into one genome.

Different Analogy. Same Biology.

Genome

Your complete set of genetic information.

DNA

The molecule that stores genetic information using A, T, C and G.

Chromosome

A packaged structure of DNA and proteins containing many genes.

Gene

A stretch of DNA containing information used to make a functional product.

Genetic variant

A difference in DNA sequence. Most variants are not disease-causing.

Pathogenic variant

A variant with evidence showing it contributes to disease.

VUS

A variant whose relationship to disease is not yet clear.

Gene expression

The process through which genetic information is used to produce a functional product.

Important: Having a genetic variant is not automatically the same as having a genetic disorder. Meaning depends on the variant, gene, evidence, inheritance and biological context.
