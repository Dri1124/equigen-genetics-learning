What if the blueprint changes?

Neutral change

A notation differs, but the structure and function are unaffected. Many genetic variants are harmless.

Uncertain change

An unfamiliar alteration appears, but there isn't enough evidence to determine whether it affects function.

Functional change

A critical structural or system instruction is disrupted. Similarly, some genetic variants interfere with normal gene function.

Everyone has genetic variation. Most DNA differences do not cause disease. The health significance of a variant depends on the gene involved, the specific change, evidence about its effect, inheritance, and clinical context.
What does this mean for me?

Genetic risk is not the same as a diagnosis.

A genetic result can provide information about risk, inheritance, or the possible cause of a condition. It should be interpreted alongside personal and family history and appropriate medical guidance.
